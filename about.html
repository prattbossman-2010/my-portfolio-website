About Me

A journey into AI, robotics, and game development

I'm Pratt Nsiah Bossman, a senior high school student (3rd year) studying Robotics Engineering at AWASO STEM High School. I've been building projects since my early coding days, combining traditional development with cutting-edge technologies.

My journey began with HTML & CSS, then expanded into Python and C++. Along the way I picked up frameworks and tools like Tkinter for desktop apps, Arduino for robotics, and SDL & OpenGL for graphics and game development.

I leverage artificial intelligence in most of my projects to ensure accuracy and efficiency — using it for quick debugging, code analysis, research, and problem-solving.

Currently, I'm learning advanced Python, SDL, and OpenGL to build an AI and a 3D open-world fighting game. Recent projects include CloudTok, a social platform inspired by TikTok, and MotionForge, a 3D motion capture and animation tool.
